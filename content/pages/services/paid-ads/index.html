Overview

Almost every underperforming account I open has the same three problems: the structure optimises for the wrong signal, the creative is one idea in five costumes, and the measurement double-counts.

So the work runs in that order. Restructure around margin and intent. Build a creative testing system with a real hypothesis behind each variant. Then fix tracking — server-side events, offline conversion imports, a blended view that reconciles platform numbers with what actually landed in the bank.

Only after that does scaling spend make sense. Pouring budget into a broken account is the fastest way to learn an expensive lesson.

Approach

How I think about paid ads.

Every account has a ceiling, and it is usually the offer, not the targeting. Before touching bids I want your gross margin, repeat rate and payback period. Those three numbers decide what a click can be worth; without them, optimisation is decoration.

I am deliberately conservative about automation. Broad match and Performance Max are useful tools with expensive default settings — they need brand exclusions, asset-group discipline and negative lists, or they quietly harvest demand you already had and bill you for it.

Category shapes the plan. Real estate is a long, high-consideration path where lead quality beats lead volume and the CRM is the scoreboard. Fashion is creative-led and seasonal, where the winning ad is a product and casting decision more than a targeting one. Crypto is a permissions problem before it is a performance one.

Questions
What is the minimum ad budget you work with?

Below roughly ₹3–4 lakh a month (about $5,000) there isn’t enough data volume for structured testing to pay for management. Under that, a one-off audit and setup sprint gives far better value than a retainer.

Do you charge a percentage of ad spend?

No. Percentage-of-spend quietly rewards spending more, which is the opposite of what you hired me for. Management is a flat monthly fee based on account complexity and number of platforms.

Can you run ads for crypto or token products?

Yes. It requires certification on some platforms, pre-cleared creative, careful landing page compliance and a realistic view of which geographies are actually servable — plus a heavier weighting toward owned and earned channels, because platform policy will change again.

Who owns the ad accounts?

You do, always. Every account, pixel, tag and dataset sits under your business manager. If we stop working together you keep the full history, the creative library and the documentation.

Can you fix tracking as well as run ads?

Yes, and it is usually first. GA4, server-side tagging, consent mode and offline conversion imports are part of setup, not an add-on — ads optimised on broken data optimise towards the wrong people.
