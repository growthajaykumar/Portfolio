Questions
Can I hire you for just one channel?

Yes — plenty of engagements are SEO-only or paid-only. What I won’t do is run a channel while quietly ignoring a problem elsewhere in the funnel. If your ads are fine and your landing page is the issue, you’ll hear it in week two.

Do you replace an agency or work alongside one?

Both happen. I often act as the strategist and quality check over an existing agency, which usually makes that agency measurably better within a quarter. Where the relationship is beyond saving, I’ll help you run a replacement search.

What is the minimum commitment?

A diagnosis sprint stands alone with no commitment beyond it. Retainers run on a three-month initial term, because anything shorter cannot honestly show whether the strategy works.

Who actually does the work?

I do. For specialist production — design, development, video, high-volume content — I bring in a small trusted bench, but strategy, analysis and account ownership never get handed off.

Do you handle restricted categories like crypto?

Yes. Crypto, token products and financial services carry real platform restrictions, and most buyers avoid them for that reason. Compliant account structures, pre-cleared creative and the right mix of owned and earned channels are part of the standard scope, not an upsell.
