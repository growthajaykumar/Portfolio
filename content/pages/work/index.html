Questions
Why are the client names not shown?

Most of this work sits under NDA or involves competitive strategy the client would rather not publish — particularly in Web3, where launch mechanics are the moat. On a call I can walk you through the accounts, dashboards and specific decisions, including references from clients happy to speak.

Are these results typical?

No result is typical — that’s the honest answer. These are engagements that went well, chosen because they show the method. I’ve also had campaigns that failed, and I’ll happily tell you about those on a call, because what went wrong is usually more instructive.

Three very different industries — doesn’t that mean shallow expertise?

Each one is technically different in a way the others could not have taught me. Web3 forced me to understand rendering and on-chain data. Real estate forced me to understand CRM and attribution across a twelve-month cycle. Fashion forced me to understand feeds, platforms and creative velocity. The vocabulary changes every time; the diagnosis does not.

Yes. Once we’re past a first conversation and there’s a real fit, I’ll connect you with clients at a comparable stage and in a comparable market.
