The idea

Most marketing plans don’t fail. They die in the queue of manual work behind them.

The strategy is sound. Then somebody has to route the leads, chase the follow-up, pull the report and tag the campaign — by hand, every week, because nobody budgeted an engineer to automate it.

I build that layer myself. n8n workflows that move data between your ad accounts, your CRM and your CMS without anyone copying a spreadsheet, and AI agents that qualify a lead, draft a reply or flag an anomaly before a person has to look at it.

I am not a software engineer and I won’t pretend to be one. What I build is the automation layer between the plan and the result — the part most marketing consultants can only tell you that you need.

How it works

A process built to kill bad ideas early.

Two weeks inside your analytics, ad accounts, search console and CRM. You get a written diagnosis of where growth is actually stuck — not a ninety-slide deck about your industry.

Kind words

What clients say.

Questions
How technical are you, exactly?

I don’t write production software, and I’ll tell you that before you have to ask. What I do build, myself, is the automation layer: n8n workflows that connect your ad accounts, CRM and CMS, and AI agents that qualify leads, draft replies or flag anomalies without a human in the loop. I’m also fluent enough in your stack to hold a real conversation with your engineers when something needs to sit deeper in the product.

Why does automation matter when hiring a marketer?

Because most marketing budget is spent on work a workflow could do for free. Routing leads, pulling reports, tagging campaigns, chasing follow-up — a marketer who cannot automate that will spend your retainer doing it by hand, or won’t notice it’s costing you at all.

Do you only work with Web3, real estate and fashion companies?

Those are where most of the last eight years went, and they are useful range: one technical market, one data-and-CRM market, one platform-and-creative market. I take work outside them when the shape of the problem is familiar. If your category is genuinely unlike anything I have touched, I will say so on the first call rather than bill you for my learning curve.

Do you work as a consultant or run campaigns hands-on?

Both, depending on what you need. Some clients want a fractional head of growth who sets strategy and manages an in-house team; others want me executing SEO and paid campaigns directly. Most engagements open with a diagnosis sprint and then move into retained execution.

How long before we see results?

Paid media usually shows signal in two to four weeks. Conversion work compounds from the first shipped test. SEO is slower and more honest: meaningful movement in three to four months, compounding returns from month six. If your timeline and your channel choice do not match, I will say so before we start.

What does an engagement cost?

Engagements are scoped by outcome, not by hours. A diagnosis sprint, a monthly retainer and a fractional growth-lead arrangement are the three common shapes. We agree the scope and the number on the first call, before there is a proposal.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. Roughly half of the work is with teams in the US, UK, UAE and Singapore, all remote — Web3 especially tends to be distributed by default. I keep a real overlap window with wherever the core team sits.
